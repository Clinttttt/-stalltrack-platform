Create the platform operator.

The platform operator administers assessment, onboarding, validation and activation for the municipalities of the CARCANMADCARLAN cluster. The account is held separately from the accounts of each municipality's Head, and is created once.

Being a separate account, it requires its own username and its own email address. An address already recorded for a Head or for a member of staff cannot be used.

Available only until the first operator exists. Activity is recorded for audit.
