Audit fixes: an LGU's receipts were about to be branded "Region XIII"

An independent review of today's commits found a real bug in my own work, and it is the kind that reaches paper.

I repurposed the assessment form's "requesting office" from an office NAME to the municipality's address. The activation mapper
still read that field as an office name, and parseOffice takes the first parenthetical as the acronym - so
"Caraga Region (Region XIII)" made "Region XIII" the office acronym written into the LGU's branding, with the whole address as its
office name. Worse, because an acronym WAS found, the "no office acronym" warning that would have shown the operator never fired.

The mapper now falls back to that field only when it still holds an office name. Requests recorded before the change do, and must
keep working, so the test recognises the shape this platform itself generates - every generated address ends with the country -
rather than guessing at addresses in general. Where it is an address, the field is ignored and the operator gets the warning.

Three specs, and restoring the old fallback fails one of them. The gap existed because nothing asserted this output at all.

Also from the audit, smaller and all verified by reading:
  - A STRAY "spaces" was rendering in the modal header, right after "LGU Assessment Request". Pre-existing, not mine, and visible
    on the page.
  - The errors signal was dead - every writer went when the two selects did, and the template never read it.
  - assessment-select.ts had no importer left. Deleted, because an unused select invites someone to "fix" this form by putting a
    dropdown back.
  - The class comment still said the form collects authorization STATUS and validates non-native selects on submit. It does
    neither; it now describes what the form actually does.

One audit finding I am NOT acting on, with the reason: the "Authorization & readiness" heading was called orphaned, but it still
labels the consent checkbox that confirms authorization will be provided. It is doing its job.
